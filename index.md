A refund also cancels your software access and voids your existing projects. Screenshot your setup steps and support messages from day one.

## Amazon's AI rules (the part most people skip)

Read [Amazon's official KDP content guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390) yourself. In summary, as of 2026:

- KDP allows AI-generated books, but you must disclose AI-generated text, images and translations when you publish.
- The disclosure is a checkbox in the publishing form and is not shown to readers.
- AI-generated text still counts as AI-generated even if you edit it lightly.
- Enforcement has tightened, with reports of books removed and accounts suspended for repeat problems.
- Amazon limits how many new titles you can create per day.
- You are responsible for quality, accuracy and copyright.

## Pre-purchase checklist

- [ ] I understand my books will be AI-generated and I must disclose that to Amazon

- [ ] I know Amazon's daily title limits apply to me

If you can tick most of these, the low price makes KDPGlobal a reasonable experiment.
