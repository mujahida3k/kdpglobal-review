# KDPGlobal in 60 Seconds

Quick answers about KDPGlobal, an AI tool sold on JVZoo that claims to write, translate and publish Kindle books and audiobooks. I have not tested the software myself, so this is a summary of the official sales page and Amazon's public rules, not a hands-on review.

## The short version

- **Price:** about $17 one-time, with an optional add-on around $10. The page quotes a regular price of $497 one-time or $197 per month.
- **Promise:** niche research, a full AI-written book, cover, audiobook narration, 10 languages and publishing to up to 21 Amazon stores.
- **Catch 1:** the 30-day refund is conditional, and a refund cancels your access.
- **Catch 2:** Amazon requires you to disclose AI-generated text, images and translations, and it enforces this.
- **Catch 3:** income figures on the page are marketing claims. The page's own disclaimer says results are not guaranteed.

## Who it is for

It may suit someone who wants a cheap test of AI-assisted KDP publishing, will edit every book, and understands there is no guaranteed income. It does not suit anyone planning to mass-publish unedited books.

## Questions people ask

**Is it legit?** It is a real offer sold through JVZoo. The earnings examples are unverified.

**Is it a subscription?** The sales page says it is a one-time payment.

**Does Amazon allow AI books?** Yes, with disclosure. Undisclosed AI content can lead to removal.
